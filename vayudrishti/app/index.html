How the demo works

Three model inputs: Open-Meteo Best Match, NOAA GFS and DWD ICON. The uncorrected blend is their equal-weight mean. Available rainfall = large-scale rain + convective showers; snow is excluded.

Regime scores (normalized to weights): dry = 1 + 3 if mean rainfall < 1 mm; depression-like = 1 + 3 if mean pressure < 1005 hPa and wind > 15 km/h; orographic proxy = 1 + 3 if API elevation > 700 m and humidity > 75%; monsoon-like = 1 + 3 if humidity > 75% and mean rainfall ≥ 1 mm.

Illustrative multipliers: dry 0.90, depression-like 1.10, orographic 1.15, monsoon-like 1.05. Blended factor = weighted mean; adjusted factor = 1 + strength × (factor − 1). These hand-picked factors are not learned bias corrections.

Agreement proxy = max(0, 1 − model range / max(10 mm, model mean)). A demo flag needs all inputs, agreement ≥ 70%, and corrected 24h rainfall ≥ 64.5 mm. Disagreement triggers abstention. Agreement is not forecast accuracy or event probability.

All calculations are local calendar-day totals (00:00–24:00 at the location), not IMD's operational observation window. Prototype thresholds: heavy ≥ 64.5 mm, very heavy ≥ 115.6 mm, extreme ≥ 204.5 mm. These are demo bins, not official alerts.
